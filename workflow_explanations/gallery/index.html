04Read the numbers carefully

Measurement notes

Token and cost basis

Input tokens include cached input; the cached column is a subset of input, not an extra amount. Costs are API-equivalent USD, not subscription bills. They include Main, workers, and corrections.

Delivery time

Seconds cover full delivery, including work by Main and workers and corrections. The batch section has one three-page delivery per method.

Limits

Model outputs are stochastic. Single-page Workflow figures are means of three runs against one fixed Skill trial per topic. The displayed measurements describe these samples only; no quality score was measured.
